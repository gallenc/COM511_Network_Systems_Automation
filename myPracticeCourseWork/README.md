# Folder containing students own work

This folder is reserved for the students own work.

You can copy any of the sessions or exercises into this folder and modify them as you choose before committing them to git.

There is also a structure for your own [Personal Journal](./personal_journal) where you can store screenshots and linked pages related to your learnings each week.

---

**NOTE**
THIS IS WHERE YOU PUT YOUR OWN WORK. DO NOT CHANGE THE REST OF THE REPOSITORY

When you fork your repository, you should put all of your exercises in separate folders within this folder. 
Make any changes here and NOT in the rest of the repository.
This will allow you to easily merge later class examples into your repository.
